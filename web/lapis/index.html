In review

Identify rocks and crystals with one photo.

Point your camera at a stone, a tumbled crystal or a rough find from a hike. Lapis names it, rates its confidence, and builds a specimen card you can keep in your collection.

App Store (in review)Questions

Lapis is for curiosity and collecting. Crystal meanings are presented as lore, not medical or financial advice.

What it does

  • Instant AI identificationSnap a photo or pick one from your library. Lapis returns the name, category and common aliases with a confidence score.
  • A full specimen cardPhysical properties, how it forms, a typical value range, care and cleaning tips, and a fun fact for every stone.
  • Meaning and loreThe stories and traditional associations people have attached to each crystal, clearly framed as lore rather than fact.
  • Your collection, on your phoneEvery identification is saved to a local collection. No account, no sign-in, nothing uploaded except the photo you choose to identify.
  • Two free scans, then Lapis ProTry it on two specimens for free. Unlimited identifications come with a weekly or yearly Lapis Pro subscription.

Questions

How accurate is the identification?

Lapis uses a vision model trained on a very wide range of minerals and is right most of the time on common stones. It shows a confidence score for every result, and it is not a substitute for a lab test or a professional appraisal.

Does Lapis value my rocks?

It shows a typical retail range for similar specimens as a rough guide only. It is not an appraisal and should not be used for buying, selling or insurance decisions.

What happens to my photos?

The photo you submit is sent to our identification service, processed, and not kept. Your collection and history stay on your device.
