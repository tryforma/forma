Coming soon

Count steps, not calories.

Stride is a gentle step tracker for people who want to walk their way to feeling better without logging a single meal. No streaks, no leaderboard, no number you have to announce. Just steps, going up.

Coming soon to the App StoreGet the release note

Stride is a tracking tool, not medical advice. If you are thinking about medication or have a health condition, talk to a doctor. StaveMD is a physician-led telehealth service in our studio family.

What it will do

  • One number, going upSteps today, steps this week. No calories, no macros, no food photos, ever.
  • No streaks to breakMiss a day and nothing resets. Stride shows a rolling average, not a chain you are afraid of snapping.
  • Walking counts, all of itThe parking lot, the pacing on a phone call, the ten minutes after lunch. It all lands in the same number.
  • Private by defaultNo account, no server, no leaderboard. Your steps stay on your phone.
  • Gentle nudges, if you want themOne optional reminder after your chosen meal: a short walk, then done.

Questions

Does Stride count calories?

No. Stride never asks about food. It counts steps and shows a simple trend. If you later want to look at what you eat, Nibble is a separate app for that, when you are ready.

Is walking enough to lose weight?

For many people a daily walk is the most sustainable place to start, especially alongside advice from a doctor. Stride is a tracking tool, not medical advice; talk to a clinician about what is right for you.

When is it coming out?

Stride is in development and has not been submitted to the App Store yet. Follow the Forma Lounge for the release note.
