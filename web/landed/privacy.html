Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Landed ("the app") is operated by Forma ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and a hashed password, used to create and secure your account (via Google Firebase Authentication).
  • Self-reported wellness data: the medication you selected, time on it, weight entries, hunger/food-noise ratings, and habit check-ins you log. This stays on your device and in your account.
  • Payment data: subscriptions are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. We store only your subscription status.
  • Coach messages: when you use the AI coach, your message and relevant protocol context are sent to our server and to our AI provider (OpenAI) to generate a reply. Coach conversations are not used to train AI models.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your data. Ever.
  • We do not share your health inputs with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not show third-party ads.

Service providers

We use Google Firebase (authentication), Stripe (payments), Vercel (hosting) and OpenAI (coach replies). Each receives only the minimum needed to provide its function.

Retention & deletion

You can clear on-device data in Settings → Delete my data. To delete your account and all server-side data, email tryformaapp@gmail.com from your account email — we complete deletion within 30 days.

Not medical care

Landed provides behavioral and educational content only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Children

Landed is not intended for anyone under 17.

Changes & contact

We'll update this page when the policy changes. Questions: tryformaapp@gmail.com.
