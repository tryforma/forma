Landed
Life after GLP-1

You lost the weight.
Now stick the landing.

Most people who stop a GLP-1 put the weight back on within a year, and faster than they expected. Landed is the quiet system for the part after: a radar that catches the drift early, a twelve-week plan that keeps you steady, and no number you have to beat except last month’s.

Download on the App Store — launching this week

Built for the off-ramp

📡 Rebound Radar

A 30-second daily check-in feeds an early-warning score that catches appetite rebound and weight drift weeks before the scale confirms it.

🪂 12-week landing protocol

Personalized to your medication, how long you took it, and your biggest fear — protein floors, hunger-wave drills, muscle anchors and stress rehearsals.

💬 A coach who gets it

Ask anything at 9pm when the food noise is loud. Grounded in post-GLP-1 physiology, never judgmental, always available.

Why now

Coming off semaglutide or tirzepatide flips powerful biology against you: hunger hormones rebound, fullness fades, metabolism runs slightly slower. Willpower isn't a plan. Structure is.

Landed is a behavioral companion, not medical care. Always follow your prescriber's guidance about medication.
