You are not broken. You are starved.

Eastwind is for men who have fought lust and porn for years, quit and gone back, and are tired of apps that count their failures. It does not count. It does not block. It does not lecture. Why the counters fail

Read the one-star reviews of the big quit-porn apps and the same three things come up: a paywall before any help, a streak that reset and took the will to keep going with it, and the feeling of opening up to someone who turns it into a lecture. They are built on subtraction: count the days, block the sites, hold the line. Men who have done that for years know exactly where it ends.

Eastwind is built on the opposite idea. The thing you reach for at night is hunger for something real: a woman, a friend, sun on your face, a night off. Nothing is missing in you. Something is missing around you, and that is easier to fix.

What it does

  • One real thingNot a discipline, a life act: send the message, ask her for coffee, call the friend, eat lunch outside, take the night off. Any size counts.
  • When it is loudNinety seconds of company, a breath, and the question that matters: what is this actually hungry for? Touch, company, rest, excitement, escape. Each one has a real move.
  • ScenesLie down and live a short scene as if it already happened: the table, Sunday morning, the walk home, her hand. First person, present tense, then sleep on it.
  • TalkAn older brother who went through this. No name, no title, no program. Ask him what to text her, whether to ask, what last night was really about, when to rest.
  • Go on the datePermission, then the practical part: the profile checklist, prompts in your voice, the first message, asking, the date, and after. Four minutes of calm before you leave.
  • The harvestThe only thing the app counts: warmth that happened. A sun that rises with three weeks of real life and never goes back to zero.

Who it is for

The man who has been meal prepping, saving, training and building a business while the evenings stayed empty. Who is burned out enough that the screen feels like the only relief. Who wants a woman in his life and is scared to ask, to be seen, to be bad at it. Eastwind gives him permission and a job for today, and it never asks what day he is on.

The name: when the sea parted, the man just held his hand up. An east wind did the work all night.
