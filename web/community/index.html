Weekly notes

  • Sep 4, 2026
    Wisp and Tomorrow You submitted

    Two apps went to App Review today. Wisp is a quit vaping companion that glows brighter every clean hour; Tomorrow You is a drink less app with a morning check-in that builds your own hangover curve. Both are account-free and keep everything on your phone.

  • Sep 3, 2026
    Lapis and MenoMind submitted

    Lapis, the rock and crystal identifier, went into review with its first build. MenoMind, a menopause companion, was submitted the same day. Two very different audiences, same rule: no account required.

  • Sep 2, 2026
    Landed submitted for review

    Landed: Life After GLP-1 is in App Review. It is the maintenance companion for people coming off Ozempic, Wegovy, Zepbound or Mounjaro, with Rebound Radar to catch regain drift early and a 12-week landing protocol.
