Batch 01 · Fall 2026 · shipping weekly

A small studio in California. Est. 2026.

You were never behind. There was never a line.

There are more of you than anyone has thought to count: the ones with the group chat from the freshman dorm still open, who can recite everyone else's title and offer year and closing date and would have to check their own, who did every item on the list and arrived to find the list had moved. Nobody built the scoreboard you have been losing on. It assembled itself out of a feed and eight people's best week, and it has never once shown the whole of anybody. It is not a measurement. It is a screenshot.

The world is a great deal larger than the eight people you measure yourself against, and there is a place in it cut to your exact size. God made it before anyone had an opinion, and He is not comparing.

The one that is not here yet

Every app above began as one person's ordinary week. If yours is not on the list, describe it in three lines and hand the prompt to Claude Code. It scaffolds the app, wires the subscriptions, writes the listing, builds it and submits it. You sign in to a few sites first; it does the rest.

The prompt

    

  

  
Before you hand it over

  
    
    
  1. Install Claude Code.It runs in the terminal. Open it in an empty folder named after your app.
    2. 
    
  2. Turn on Claude in Chrome.Install the Chrome extension and sign in. This is how it clicks through the sites for you instead of asking you to.
    3. 
    
  3. Sign in to these, in Chrome, before you paste anything.Apple Developer Program (the one thing that costs money, $99 a year) · App Store Connect · Expo for the builds · RevenueCat for the subscriptions · Supabase if anything must sync · GitHub · Vercel for the one-page site.
    4. 
    
  4. Paste the prompt. Answer its two questions. Go for a walk.It will stop for the things only you can do: agreeing to Apple's paid-app agreement and entering banking and tax details. Do those yourself; never hand anyone those.

  What we are against
  
We are told a lot of things

  
    
    
  • That you are behind, on a timeline nobody ever wrote down and which is somehow still binding.
    • 
    
  • That the eight people in the group chat are the sample, and that the sample is the world.
    • 
    
  • That the title is the man, and that the man can be read off a profile in under a second.
    • 
    
  • That wanting a quieter, smaller life is settling, and that settling is a failure rather than a decision.
    • 
    
  • That you should be realistic, a word which in practice has only ever meant smaller.
    • 
  

  
None of it is true. Not one line of it. And most of the people who seem to be winning by it are checking the same tab you are.

  
What we believe

  
  • We believe you were made on purpose, before anyone had an opinion.
  • We believe there is no leaderboard, and that the feeling of one is the most expensive thing you own.
  • We believe the world is enormous, and that this is a relief.
  • We believe enough is a number, and that you have probably already passed it.
  • We believe a life the size of one person is a whole life, and not a consolation prize.
  • We believe being known beats being looked at, every time.
  • We believe your data belongs on your phone, and that every support email should be read and answered by a person. Ours are.
