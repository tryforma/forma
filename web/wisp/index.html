In review

Quit vaping with a companion that glows brighter every clean hour.

When you start, a small cloud-spirit is born. Every clean hour makes it brighter. Vape again and it dims for a day, and that is all. No streak wiped to zero, no guilt screen. Most people quit in loops, not lines. Wisp is built for that.

App Store (in review)Questions

Wisp is a motivational companion, not medical advice. Talk to a healthcare professional about quitting, especially if you are pregnant, under 18, or have a medical condition.

What it does

  • Your Wisp, your progressIt breathes, blinks and floats on your home screen, moving from Grey to Pale, Sky, Mint, Golden and Radiant as your clean hours add up.
  • Slips dim, they never resetTap "I slipped" and your Wisp dims for a day. Lifetime clean days, money saved and puffs avoided keep counting.
  • Craving SOS in three minutes60 seconds of box breathing, 60 seconds of a tiny catch-the-Wisp game, 60 seconds with the reasons you wrote down. Then the craving passes.
  • Taper at your pacePick a 7, 14 or 30 day taper with a daily puff allowance that shrinks gently to zero, and a one-tap puff counter.
  • Watch your body bounce backA recovery timeline from 20 minutes to one year, plus money saved, puffs avoided, cravings beaten and achievements.

A look inside

Questions

Do I have to quit cold turkey?

No. Wisp supports a gradual taper with a daily puff allowance if that is what works for you, or a clean quit date if you are ready.

What happens when I slip?

Your Wisp dims for a day and that is it. Your lifetime stats keep counting and nothing resets to zero. Slips are part of quitting.

Is my data private?

Yes. There is no account, no sign-in and no server. Your quit date, slips and stats stay on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.
