Ascension

A plan for the frame and the face, and a few people who notice.

Ascension is not a score. It is an honest read of what would actually help, a daily plan of low-risk habits that add up, and a small squad that sees when you show up and says so lightly. The only comparison it ever draws is with last month’s you.

Download on theApp Store
01 · Scan

Honest, not brutal

A fixed rubric rates skin, hair, jawline, eyes, grooming, style and physique — the same way every time, so progress is real.

02 · Plan

Low-risk levers only

Skincare, hair, grooming, training, posture, sleep, style and social reps. No surgery, no drugs, no extreme diets. Ever.

03 · Squad

Accountability that bites

Daily check-ins and streaks shared with a few guys doing the same work. Miss a day and they'll know.

Ascension is rated 17+. Scores and plans are AI-generated opinions about appearance, not medical advice. Photos are used only to generate your scan and are never stored on our servers.
