A reading to begin the day, and one to end it.

Evensong is a daily Christian devotional. Every morning and every evening, all year, a short reading from Spurgeon’s Morning and Evening, the verse it rests on, and a one-line prayer. Before sleep, three quiet questions. No account, no feed, nothing to keep up with.

Coming soon to the App StoreGet the release note

Evensong is a devotional companion, not therapy or medical care. If you are in crisis, please contact local emergency services or a crisis line.

What it will do

  • Two readings a day, the whole yearCharles Spurgeon’s classic Morning and Evening (1865), lightly updated for modern readers. A page in the morning, a page at night, 365 days.
  • The verse it rests onEach reading opens with its verse from the World English Bible and closes with a one-line prayer you can actually say.
  • An evening examenThree questions before sleep: what am I grateful for, where did I fall short, what is one thing for tomorrow. Saved to a private journal on your phone.
  • Bookmarks and a year calendarKeep the readings that stayed with you. See the days you read at a glance. Miss a day and nothing resets; the next page is simply there.
  • Reminders at your hoursA morning and an evening reminder at the times you choose. Local notifications only, nothing sent anywhere.
  • Made for readingAdjustable serif text. A parchment page by day, a candlelit page at night.

Free and Pro

  • Free, every dayToday’s morning reading with its verse and prayer, and the evening examen. Enough to keep the habit on its own.
  • ProEvening readings, the full year, journal history, bookmarks, reminders and text size. $7.99 a month or $39.99 a year, both with a 7-day free trial. Billed by Apple; cancel any time.
  • Private by designNo account and no server. Your journal, bookmarks and reading days live on your phone. Delete the app and they are gone.

Questions

Is Evensong religious?

Yes, plainly. Evensong is a Christian devotional built around Charles Spurgeon’s Morning and Evening and the Bible. It is warm rather than strict, and Christians of many traditions have read Spurgeon for 160 years. If you would rather have a secular daily ritual, Lantern is the sister app for that.

Where do the readings come from?

From Charles Spurgeon’s Morning and Evening (1865), which is in the public domain, lightly updated for modern readers without changing the substance. The verse each reading rests on comes from the World English Bible, also public domain. The one-line prayers are original.

What is an evening examen?

An old Christian practice of looking back over the day before sleep. Evensong keeps it to three questions: what am I grateful for, where did I fall short, and what is one thing for tomorrow. Your answers are saved to a private journal on your phone.

What is free and what is Pro?

Today’s morning reading and the evening examen are free, every day. Pro adds the evening readings, the full year, journal history, bookmarks, reminders and text size: $7.99 a month or $39.99 a year, both with a 7-day free trial.

When is it coming out?

Evensong is in development and has not been submitted to the App Store yet. Follow the Forma Lounge for the release note.
