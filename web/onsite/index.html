Onsite

Coming soon

You are closer than you think.

Round-by-round preparation for the interview loops at Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Meta and Big Tech. What each round actually tests, a plan built backwards from your date, an AI interviewer that asks the follow-ups, and a debrief written the way a real one is written. Preparation, not a verdict; nobody here is ranking you against the other candidates.

为 Anthropic、OpenAI、DeepMind 等公司面试准备。真实轮次拆解、按你的面试日期倒推的周计划、会追问的 AI 面试官、以及像真实 debrief 一样的评分卡。中文解析全程可开。

Independent and not affiliated with any company named here. Preparation only, never live assistance. No offer is guaranteed.

What is inside

  • The loops, round by roundAnthropic's four-level coding round and values conversation. OpenAI's ML coding from scratch and debugging. DeepMind's fundamentals quiz and research critique. What each one tests, the themes that recur, and how to prepare for it specifically.
  • A plan built to your dateTell it when the interview is and where you are weak. You get a week-by-week plan that front-loads the round you are worst at, with concrete tasks and scheduled rest.
  • Mock rounds with follow-upsNot a question list. An interviewer that pushes on the vague parts, asks for numbers, and goes one level deeper, then ends the round like a real one.
  • A real debrief scorecardStrong Hire, Hire, Lean No Hire, No Hire. Scored by dimension, with what worked, what was missing, and the three drills to do this week.
  • Drills that come backA graded question bank on spaced repetition. Cards you answer weakly return tomorrow; cards you nail return in weeks.
  • Stories, sharpenedYour STAR stories rewritten into two-minute answers, in your own facts. Nothing invented, gaps marked for you to fill.

Free and Pro

  • FreeYour plan, one full mock round with its scorecard, ten graded drills, and one sharpened story.
  • Onsite ProUnlimited mocks and scorecards, the full drill bank with grading and spaced repetition, and unlimited story sharpening. $19.99 a week with a 3-day free trial, or $149.99 a year. Billed by Apple; cancel any time.
  • PrivateNo account. Your plan, transcripts, grades and stories live on your phone. Text you submit is used for that one response and not stored.

The honest part

No app gets you the offer. The reps get you the offer, and the reps are yours: the mornings before work, the problems you did not want to do, the story you rewrote six times. What Onsite can do is make sure you are practising the right things in the right format, and tell you the truth about where you stand instead of flattering you. That is the whole promise, and we would rather keep it than make a bigger one.

没有 app 能给你 offer，能给你 offer 的是你自己的练习量。我们能做的是保证你练的是对的东西，并且对你说实话。这就是全部的承诺。

Questions

Is this affiliated with Anthropic or OpenAI?

No. Onsite is completely independent and not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any company named in the app. Loop descriptions are compiled from public interview guides, company posts and candidate reports on forums like 1point3acres and Xiaohongshu.

Does it help me cheat in a live interview?

No, and it never will. Onsite is preparation before the interview. Real-time answer assistance during an interview breaks employer rules and gets offers rescinded. We do not build it.

Will it get me the offer?

No app can. Your reps get you the offer. What Onsite does is make sure you are practising the right rounds in the right format, and tell you honestly where you stand instead of flattering you.

What does the mock interview actually do?

It plays the interviewer for one round: it asks the opening question, then follows up on vague answers the way a real interviewer does, and after six to eight exchanges it writes a debrief scorecard on the four-point scale (Strong Hire to No Hire), by dimension, with your gaps named and the next drills chosen.

Is there Chinese support?

Yes. Chinese coaching notes can be turned on in onboarding or the Me tab, and they appear under every scorecard, drill grade and loop round. The interview practice itself is in English, because the interviews are.

What is free and what is Pro?

Your plan, one full mock round with its scorecard, ten graded drills and one sharpened story are free. Onsite Pro adds unlimited mocks, the full drill bank with grading and spaced repetition, and unlimited story sharpening: $19.99 a week with a 3-day free trial, or $149.99 a year.
